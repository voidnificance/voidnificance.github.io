Modular PCB fixture

Two fixed datum edges locate the board. Slotted fasteners and a compliant jaw accommodate width variation while leaving the board face accessible.

Assembly components

CAD checks

Verified support and datum contacts, clamp-hole clearance, and zero solid interference for both configurations. Camera and lens selection are pending.

Width tolerance

An assumed ±0.15 mm board width, ±0.10 mm datum location and ±0.10 mm clamp setup give a ±0.35 mm worst-case edge offset. This estimate informs the required jaw travel.

    Sheet-Diamond lattice

    How does porosity change the compressive response of the same lattice topology?

    Contribution
    Co-authored the study and contributed to specimen preparation, mechanical characterization, and interpretation of deformation and fracture.
    Published result
    The low-porosity PLA group had about 3× the yield strength and 2.5× the modulus of the high-porosity group. The groups showed different failure modes.
    Reconstructed model
    The portfolio reconstruction uses an implicit Diamond surface clipped to a 20 mm cube. The 2 × 2 × 2 repeats and target porosities are demonstration settings. The STL exports were checked for watertightness and connectivity.

    University of Toronto · M.Eng. project

    Conductive adhesive coupon

    A lap-joint specimen for measuring electrically conductive adhesive in shingled photovoltaic interconnects.

    Design decision
    Used a PCB lap joint with separate current and voltage-sense connections to keep lead resistance out of the voltage reading. The model uses the 20 × 15 mm PCB outline in the report.
    Experimental work
    Ran cyclic tests on six silicon-based specimens with copper grip extensions. The measurements showed differing resistance trends and exposed limits in acquisition speed and specimen handling.
    CAD reconstruction
    The STEP assembly shows the proposed PCB concept. Board stack-up, routing details, 3.50 mm overlap, and 0.10 mm adhesive thickness are modeled assumptions.

    Portfolio design study · Parametric CAD

    Coupon handling nest

    A bench fixture concept for locating the reconstructed PCB coupon during assembly and inspection.

    Design
    Two pocket depths accommodate the offset lap joint. A central window exposes the bond area and removable keepers sit above the two coupon ends.
    Manufacturing approach
    The base is a CNC acetal concept with reliefs at the pocket corners and through bores accessible from one setup. The nominal pocket allows 0.25 mm lateral clearance per side.
    Verification
    Checked the envelope, seat clearance, access window, bore paths, and assembly interference in CAD. The concept was created with AI assistance for this portfolio and remains unbuilt.

    Carnegie Mellon University · Ph.D. research

    Aluminum recrystallization

    Tracking grain and subgrain evolution in cold-rolled, high-purity aluminum during stepwise annealing.

    Contribution
    Designed and performed repeated EBSD mapping and developed a workflow combining dictionary indexing and MATLAB orientation processing.
    What to look for
    The inverse pole figure map (IPF-Z) uses color to distinguish orientations along the sample normal. In the orientation similarity map (OSM), track the bright recrystallized grains as they grow into the textured deformed matrix.
    Sequence
    Both views show the same field of view at 15 stages, from the initial state (t0) through 14 annealing steps at 200 °C (t1–t14).
